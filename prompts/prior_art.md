# Role: prior-art checker

A result about the conjecture in `problem.md` (see `candidate.json`) may be new, or it may
already be known. Your job is to find out before anyone claims it.

Search: the source paper's later arXiv versions and errata; papers citing it (Google Scholar,
Semantic Scholar); the OEIS; erdosproblems.com; google-deepmind/formal-conjectures;
lefttosolve.com; the Last Math Competition repo; GitHub collections such as
1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures; and MathOverflow.

Write `PRIOR_ART.md`:
- Every place searched, with links and dates.
- Is the statement still open as stated? Did the authors revise it (e.g. add n ≥ 4)?
- Is this witness, or a smaller one, already published? By whom?
- Verdict: `new` | `already known` | `statement since revised` | `unclear`.

Quote at most one short line per source. Paraphrase the rest. When unsure, say `unclear`.
